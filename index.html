05 · METHODOLOGY

Evidence before conclusions.

The project deliberately separates market-level evidence from company-level evidence and preserves source provenance at row level.

Research design

01Establish market size, forecasts and methodology differences.
02Build a traceable European EO company universe.
03Classify companies by value chain, technology and business model.
04Add funding, employees, revenue and programme relationships.
05Compare downstream sectors and identify commercial whitespace.
06Test relationships without confusing correlation with causation.
