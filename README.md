# European Earth Observation Market 2026
## From Open Data to Commercial Intelligence

https://marcoleonardi97.github.io/eueomarket/

**Research question:** How is value created across Europe's Earth observation industry, and where are the strongest opportunities for European commercial growth?

### Repository structure

- `data/raw/` — company, funding, market and programme datasets
- `data/reference/` — data dictionary and source register
- `notebooks/` — reproducible analysis notebooks
- `src/` — reusable analysis code
- `figures/` — exported charts
- `report/` — final strategic report

### Current state

Dataset v0.1 contains a deliberately traceable seed of European commercial EO companies drawn from ESA/Copernicus sources. It is **not yet a representative sample** of the full European EO industry.

### Research architecture

1. Establish market size and methodology differences.
2. Build the European company universe.
3. Classify companies by EO value-chain position and technology.
4. Add funding, employees and revenue.
5. Map ESA/Copernicus relationships.
6. Analyse downstream commercialisation.
7. Score market segments and strategic opportunities.
8. Produce evidence-based implications for European space policy and industry.

### Key methodological principle

The project will not treat ESA/Copernicus participation as evidence of commercial success. It will test relationships and explicitly distinguish correlation from causation.

### Initial authoritative benchmarks

- EUSPA: 772 European EO companies, 13,796 employees and €1.796B revenue.
- EARSC: 811 companies, 16,036 employees and €2.66B revenue in its 2025 survey.
- ESA: 25 commercial data providers in the Copernicus Contributing Mission ecosystem as of June 2025, with emerging European providers joining in cohorts.

These figures have different scopes and methodologies and must therefore remain source-specific.
